About

Specs

I make pictures for people who'd rather remember a feeling than a pose.

Ten years photographing editorial stories, quiet portraits, and unglamorous documentary work across Southern Europe and North Africa. Clients include independent magazines, small design studios, and a handful of families who wanted their year kept somewhere safe.

Shoots are planned loosely and executed patiently — most of what ends up in a final edit was never staged.
